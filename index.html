PROJECT 01 / EMAIL

Morning Ritual

A welcome-email concept for a fictional coffee brand, built around the comfort of a good morning cup.

Subject: Make your morning as smooth as our coffee

Preview Text:From farm to your morning ritual.
Emotional Connection:If we have anything in common, we'll agree that there's nothing like a fresh cup of coffee each morning.
The Problem:Unfortunately…
Busy schedules.
Tight calendars.
Heavy workloads.
Leave little time to find good roasted beans!
Approach:So, we at Morning Ritual decided to take care of that, so you don't have to.
We bring you fresh, high-quality coffee beans for everyday mornings or special occasions, suited to your unique taste preferences.
Whether you prefer bold or light roasts, we've got options for you.
CTA:Explore our collection and find your next favourite.

PROJECT 02 / SALES COPY

The Daily Planner

A sales-page concept for a fictional stationery product

Headline: Your day deserves a little more direction.

The Problem:Waking up to a long to-do list isn't ideal. When that list is disorganized, it's even harder to know where to start.
You spend time switching between tasks, struggle to prioritize, and end the day wondering where your time went.
The Introduction:With our Daily Planner, you can bring more structure to your day.
Set your three daily priorities, organize your tasks, and take a moment to reflect at the end of the day.
The Benefit:Say goodbye to:
*Messy task lists
*Unclear priorities
*Losing track of your progress
Make a plan, focus on what matters, and give each day a clearer direction.
Designed to be comfortable to carry and available in three stylish colors.
CTA:Get Yours Today!

PROJECT 03 / LANDING PAGE COPY

Local Gym

A landing-page concept for a fictional local gym, focused on making strength training feel more approachable.

Headline:We lay the path, you take the steps.
Subheadline:Easy steps that build your strength.

Customer problem:Building strength is more of a psychological thing than you may think.
Open spaces, large crowds, and randomized routines can put pressure on you.
Increasing difficulty does not automatically increase results.
Resetting your progress and changing your routine is no solution.
Results come from efficient training, not just hard work.
Approach:We developed our gym with your health in mind.
Smaller groups, guided by our personal trainers.
Track your progress alongside others on a similar path.
Follow an easy-to-understand routine that helps you build strength.
CTA:Book a free session with us and see if we're a fit.

PROJECT 04 / EMAIL · RE-ENGAGEMENT

Bookstore Welcome-Back Email

A short re-engagement email for a fictional bookstore. The brief provided limited information about the store and its former customer, so the concept avoids unsupported claims about products or offers.

Subject: Your preferences matter to us.

Preview Text:Books bring out the different you.
The Reason:Reading can provide different perspectives and help you discover something new about yourself.
Just 10 minutes with a book can be a small way to make room for reading in your day.
Whenever you're ready to explore again, we'd love to welcome you back.

CTA:Browse our latest collection.
