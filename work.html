PROJECT 03 / LANDING PAGE COPY

A landing-page concept for a fictional local gym, focused on making strength training feel more approachable.

We lay the path, you take the steps.
Easy steps that build your strength.

Building strength is more of a psychological thing than you may think.
Open spaces, large crowds, and randomized routines can put pressure on you.
Increasing difficulty does not automatically increase results.
Resetting your progress and changing your routine is no solution.
Results come from efficient training, not just hard work.

We developed our gym with your health in mind.
Smaller groups, guided by our personal trainers.

Follow an easy-to-understand routine that helps you build strength.

PROJECT 05 / EMAIL · ABANDONED CART

LumaSkin — Abandoned-Cart Email Sequence

A three-email concept for a fictional skincare brand, introducing its Daily Balance Moisturizer and encouraging shoppers to return to their carts. This is a practice project, not paid client work.

Email 1 · Subject: Your skin should not miss the care it deserves.

Preview: Your skin deserves attention

Skin is the one part of our body that is exposed to everything.
Busy schedules and complicated routines make it hard to care for our skin.
So we at LumaSkin present to you our latest product!
Our Daily Balance Moisturizer, designed to keep your delicate skin hydrated and smooth.
For more information, visit our product page.

Email 2 · Subject: Make hydration a part of your daily life.

Preview: Skincare is not supposed to be hard.

Leaving your skin unattended for a long time can result in rough texture, dehydrated skin, and irritation.
This can make your skin more vulnerable to damage.
With our product, you can avoid all that!
Our Daily Balance Moisturizer is lightweight, non-greasy, and designed to be easy to use and fast-acting.
Just apply it once in the morning or evening, and it will keep your skin hydrated and smooth.
To order, click the link below.

Email 3 · Subject: Give your skin a gift.

Preview: No more dehydrated skin!

We at LumaSkin care about you, and our Daily Balance Moisturizer is designed to do just that.
In a 50 mL container, you can have the gift your skin deserves.
It's lightweight and non-greasy, giving your skin a smooth, clear finish.
Orders over $10 qualify for free delivery.
Let us be a part of your daily skincare routine.
